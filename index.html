Launch checklist

1. Practitioner authorization

Confirm the current Tennessee pathway and obtain the required massage therapist license before advertising/accepting compensated massage.

2. Establishment requirements

Confirm whether the home studio setup requires a massage establishment license and satisfy applicable requirements before opening to the public.

3. Business setup

Confirm county/city business-license, tax-registration, insurance and zoning/home-business requirements that apply to the final setup.

4. Booking launch

After authorization, activate real appointment booking, deposits/payments, intake/consent forms, cancellation policy and client reminders.
